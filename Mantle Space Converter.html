Output format rules
✓
Only offices with a real price are included — LET, STC, Store, Meeting Room, Home-Flexi, and By-the-Hour entries are all skipped automatically.
✓
Units marked "Under Offer" are skipped automatically.
✓
Units with no valid desk number (blank, N/A, TBC, 0) are skipped automatically.
✓
POA pricing: if a unit says POA, the configured "from" rate for that venue is used (e.g. Kings Court → £360, Officers' Mess → £400). Marked in the preview so you can review before downloading.
✓
Output columns: Floor · Office/Unit Number · Square footage · Desk capacity · Price per month · Floor · Min term (1) · Max term (12) · Available from · What's included · What's not included · Office position · Number of additional rooms
✓
Available from: "Available Soon" and bare "Available" → Now (blank in CSV). Any specific date is output as YYYY-MM-DD (month-only dates, e.g. "August 2026", become 2026-08-01).
✓
What's included is populated per-building from the configured amenity list — Thremhall Park, Officers' Mess, Lambourn, Bradfield, 9 Hills Road, Kings Court, and Elizabeth House each have their own list.
✓
What's not included: VAT (all venues).
✓
Excluded unit types: Meeting Room, Home-Flexi, Dedicated Desk, Store, Coworking, By the Hour, Billiard Room, Card Room, Ante Room, The Quarters, Breakout Room, Centre Manager Office.
✓
Floor labels: Ground, Lower Ground, 1st, 2nd, 3rd — detected from section headers in the PDF.
✓
Venue auto-detected from "Space Information / [Venue Name]" on the cover page. Override available if needed.
