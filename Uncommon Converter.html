Output format & rules
✓
One CSV per Uncommon building (Liverpool Street, Fulham, Borough, Highbury & Islington, Holborn). Multiple buildings → downloaded as a .zip.
✓
Output columns: Floor · Office/Unit Number · Square footage · Desk capacity · Price per month · Floor · Min term · Max term · Available from · What's included · What's not included · Office position · Number of additional rooms
✓
Unit number taken directly from the office ref after the | pipe (e.g. "42 Desks + 2 Meeting Rooms | 1.04-6" → "Office 1.04-6"). "Available Now" listings → blank available date. Future months → formatted date.
✓
Prices rounded up to nearest whole pound. VAT suffix stripped.
✓
Desk count taken from the desks cell (e.g. "42 Desks" → 42). Ranges like "4-5 Desks" → lower number.
✓
Meeting rooms extracted from desk label (e.g. "+ 2 Meeting Rooms" → 2 in additional rooms column).
✓
Notes from the availability row included in What's included.
